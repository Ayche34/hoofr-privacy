Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new trip?

Tap "Create Trip" from the My Trips screen. Enter your trip name, dates, and location. You'll get a 6-digit code to share with your group.

How do I join an existing trip?

Tap "Join Trip" and enter the 6-digit code shared by the trip host. You'll be added to the trip and can see all the details instantly.

Can I clone a previous trip?

Yes! Press and hold the trip card and you can clone a trip, archive a trip, remove yourself from a trip, or delete the trip forever.

What does archiving a trip do?

If you press and hold a trip card and archive it, it basically minimizes to the bottom of your trips page. You can press and hold again to un-archive.

How do I add players?

The trip host can add players from the Players tab — either one by one or by importing a CSV file. Players who join with a trip code are automatically added to the roster.

Can I add co-hosts to help me fill out the itinerary??

Yes, in the trip settings you can add multiple co-hosts and also re-assign ownership of the trip to someone who has joined.

Can I clone individual itinerary items?

Yes, press and hold an itinerary item, and you'll see the option to clone it.

Is there a way to confirm guests cannot see hidden items?

Yes, in your trip settings, you can toggle on guest view and see what your guests see. Go back to settings to toggle it back off.

My photo is getting cut off, can I crop it?

Yes, you'll see an up and down arrow on both the trip image and the lodging images, and you can slide the image up and down so it is better aligned.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to your Profile (tap the person icon on My Trips), scroll down, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your trip data is only visible to members who join with the trip code. We do not sell or share your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
